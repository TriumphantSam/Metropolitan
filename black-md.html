Meet Dr. Chukwuma Okoli

Bridging the gap between clinical medicine and everyday wellness through Black MD.

Chief Health Educator

Dr. Chukwuma Okoli (Black MD)

"As medical professionals, sharing actionable, evidence-based health tips online is one of the most powerful tools we have to prevent disease and protect our families before they ever need a hospital bed."

Dr. Okoli's mission with Black MD is to simplify complex clinical science into practical, daily choices. By providing verified healthcare advice on YouTube, radio, and social feeds, he helps thousands take control of their cardiovascular health, family nutrition, and lifestyle wellness.
