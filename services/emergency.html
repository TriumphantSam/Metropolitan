Emergency Medical Services

Urgent, high-standard medical intervention when every second counts. Our emergency unit and clinical personnel are prepared 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, to handle trauma and critical needs.

Call Hotline: 08064549283

Immediate clinical attention when it is needed most.

Medical emergencies can happen unexpectedly. Our emergency department at Metropolitan Family Hospital is designed for rapid triage, prompt diagnosis, and stabilization. Under the supervision of experienced emergency physicians and nurses, we offer professional stabilization for trauma, acute cardiac symptoms, severe infections, and respiratory crises.

What We Offer

01

24/7 Triage & Stabilization

Around-the-clock medical assessment to prioritize patient treatment based on severity and urgency.

02

Trauma & Accident Management

Immediate surgical stabilization, wound repair, fracture care, and critical injury coordination.

03

Fast-Track Referral Channels

Immediate access to our laboratory, pharmacy, and surgical teams to optimize patient outcomes during critical windows.
