Surgical Services

Safe, high-standard surgical care performed by experienced surgeons. Our modern operating theatre supports both emergency operations and scheduled minor and major surgeries.

Schedule Consultation

Clinical excellence and safety in our operating theatre.

Undergoing surgery requires trust, preparation, and precision. The surgical team at Metropolitan Family Hospital is dedicated to maintaining sterile environments, modern monitoring systems, and clear pre-operative assessments. From emergency caesarean sections and appendectomies to planned general procedures, we coordinate your path to recovery safely.

Surgical Capabilities

General Surgery & Emergencies

02

Obstetric Surgery

Planned and emergency Caesarean sections (C-sections) managed by experienced obstetricians and pediatric cover.

03

Post-Operative Recovery Ward

Dedicated post-op recovery care, vital signs monitoring, pain management, and surgical wound follow-up.
