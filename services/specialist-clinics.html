Specialist Clinics

Focused medical consultations with visiting and resident consultants. Access expert care in Cardiology, Paediatrics, Urology, Internal Medicine, and more.

Schedule Specialist Visit

Targeted expert clinical care for complex health needs.

Some health concerns require deeper, targeted expertise. At Metropolitan Family Hospital, we run weekly specialist clinic sessions. By coordinating with some of the most respected medical consultants in Oyo State, we bring expert cardiologists, paediatricians, and other medical specialists right to our Akobo facility, sparing you stressful trips to tertiary centers.

Key Specialist Areas

01

Paediatric Clinic

Comprehensive health support for children, covering growth tracking, developmental consultations, and childhood illness care.

02

Cardiology Clinic

Expert evaluation of heart symptoms, management of chronic cardiovascular disease, and preventative vascular checkups.

03

Internal Medicine & Endocrinology

In-depth management of hormone imbalances, thyroid issues, renal health concerns, and complex systemic conditions.
