Compassionate care for motherhood and beyond.

The Obstetrics & Gynaecology department at Metropolitan Family Hospital provides specialized, private health support for women. Our medical staff focuses on creating a comfortable, supportive environment for expectant mothers, dealing with reproductive wellness, and providing critical guidance throughout pregnancy and postpartum recovery.

What We Offer

01

Antenatal Care & Classes

Regular checkups, health tracking for mother and baby, nutritional advice, and preparatory birth sessions.

02

Safe Delivery Services

Equipped labor wards, experienced midwives, obstetric surgeons on stand-by, and comprehensive postnatal monitoring.

03

Gynaecological Consultations

Well-woman checks, diagnostic screening for reproductive concerns, family planning guidance, and menopausal health advice.
