Genuine medications and professional clinical guidance.

Getting the right medication is vital for your treatment. The pharmacy department at Metropolitan Family Hospital sources all medicines directly from verified pharmaceutical distributors. This guarantees the authenticity, active strength, and safety of all prescriptions. Our pharmacists do more than just dispense; they verify interactions, discuss potential side effects, and counsel you on correct schedules.
