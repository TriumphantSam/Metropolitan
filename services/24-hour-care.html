Dependable clinical care, day or night.

Health concerns do not follow a calendar or a clock. The commitment of Metropolitan Family Hospital to Akobo and the broader Ibadan community means our hospital never closes its doors. Our 24-hour service structure ensures that whether you walk in at midday or need emergency guidance at 3:00 AM, you will be attended to by qualified clinical staff.
